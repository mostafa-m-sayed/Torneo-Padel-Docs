Torneo Padel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Torneo Padel ("the app") is available for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: the app stores only what's needed to run your tournaments, plus anonymous crash reports and usage statistics that help us fix problems. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold.

No account, no sign-up

You don't create an account. When you first open the app, it signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication, which gives your device a random identifier. That identifier is used only to know which tournaments you created, so that only you (or guests, if you allow it) can change them. It is not linked to your name, email address or phone number.

Tournament information you enter

When you create a tournament, the app stores what you enter: the tournament name and settings, team and player names, the schedule and match scores. This information is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (part of Google Firebase) so that it syncs between devices in real time.

Anyone who has a tournament's code can view that tournament, including its player names and results. Only enter names that the players are happy to share with the people taking part.

Crash reports and usage statistics

The app (on iPhone and Android) uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to tell us when the app crashes and which features are used, so we can fix bugs and improve the app. This includes:

  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, the device model, operating system version and app version.
  • Usage statistics: events such as the app being opened and screens viewed, session length, device type, operating system version, app version and approximate country (derived from the network connection).
  • A random identifier for this installation, so reports from the same phone can be grouped. It is not your name, email or phone number, and it changes if you reinstall the app.

This data doesn't include tournament contents or player names, isn't used to identify you, and isn't used for advertising or shared with advertisers: the advertising features of Google Analytics (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization) are switched off in the app, and the app doesn't ask to track you or access your device's advertising identifier (on Android, the advertising-ID permission is removed from the app). Crash reports are kept for 90 days; usage statistics are kept for the limited retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months).

Sharing from the app

When you share a tournament code, a schedule PDF or the results picture, the app hands it to your phone's share sheet, and it goes only where you choose to send it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or renting of any information.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase: Authentication and Cloud Firestore to sign you in anonymously and store tournament data, and Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase for crash reports and usage statistics. Google processes this data on our behalf according to the Firebase privacy and security terms. Data is encrypted in transit.

Keeping and deleting data

Tournament data is kept until the tournament is deleted. Only the organizer can delete a tournament; guests, even when allowed to enter scores, cannot. Deleting it also deletes its teams and matches. You can also ask us to delete a tournament, or any data related to you, by emailing mostafa-m-sayed@hotmail.com. Uninstalling the app removes the anonymous identifier from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
