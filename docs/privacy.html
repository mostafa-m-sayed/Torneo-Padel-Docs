Torneo Padel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Torneo Padel ("the app") is built to keep things simple: it does not require an account, does not track you, and does not collect personal information beyond what you choose to type in while setting up a tournament.

Information We Collect

The app does not require sign-in or an account of any kind. The only data stored is the tournament information you enter yourself, such as:

  • Team and player names you type in
  • Match scores and standings you record
  • Tournament settings (number of teams, groups, format)

This data is stored in our database (Google Firestore) solely to power the app's tournament and scheduling features. No names or scores you enter are used for any purpose beyond displaying and calculating your tournament.

What We Don't Do

  • No account creation or sign-in required
  • No analytics or usage tracking SDKs
  • No crash reporting tools
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties

Data Storage

Tournament data you enter is stored using Google Firestore, a cloud database service. Data is stored to allow the app to function (displaying schedules, standings, and results) and is not shared with any other party.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no account or sign-in is required, and the only data entered is tournament/team names chosen by the user, no additional personal data is requested from any user regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
