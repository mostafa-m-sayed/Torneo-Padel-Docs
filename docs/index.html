Two ways to play

Pick the format at setup. The app handles the rest.

🏆 Groups → Knockout

Fixed pairs play in groups, then a seeded bracket up to 64 teams, with byes and an optional third-place match.

📊 Groups → Mini League

The qualifiers play each other once and the final table crowns the champion.

🔄 Americano

4 to 40 players, a new partner every round, the same number of matches for everyone and fair sit-outs.

🎯 Points or games

Play Americano to 16, 21, 24 or 32 points, or to a short or long set.

🥇 Final and consolation

Americano groups lead to a final, and the other players keep playing in consolation groups by level.

⚖️ Fair by design

Balanced random draws, no team playing twice in a round, and partners that don't repeat.

Everything on match day

Built for organizers on the move, and for everyone watching.

📅 Schedules and courts

Fixtures and courts are assigned automatically. Name your courts to match the venue.

⚡ Scores in seconds

Tap a match, tap a quick score, done. Standings update instantly.

📱 Follow live

Share the tournament code: players and fans follow scores and brackets on their own phones, iPhone or Android.

🔀 Change the order

Drag and drop to swap matches; the app blocks swaps that don't work.

🖨️ Table and PDF

See the whole day as rounds × courts, then print or share it as a PDF.

🎉 Share the podium

A ready-made results picture and message for WhatsApp, Instagram or the club group.
