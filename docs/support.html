Frequently Asked Questions

How many teams can I add to a tournament?

There's no limit — Torneo Padel works for small club tournaments and large competitive events alike.

Is Torneo Padel free?

Yes, the app is completely free to use, with no subscriptions or hidden fees.

How are standings calculated?

Standings update automatically as you enter match results, based on the round-robin group stage rules.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Email mostafa-m-sayed@hotmail.com with a description of what happened and, if possible, steps to reproduce it.
