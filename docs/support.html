Frequently Asked Questions

Is Torneo Padel free?

Yes. Creating and running tournaments, following them live and player accounts are free, with no ads. Some extras for organizers, like co-admins, are part of Premium.

Do I need an account?

No. You can create, run and follow tournaments without one. A free account (Apple, Google or email) adds a player profile with a username and photo, your tournaments on every phone, and joining tournaments where players ask to take part.

What's the difference between a "Tournament on the fly" and "Players request to join"?

In a Tournament on the fly the organizer types the players' names — nobody needs an account. With Players request to join, the organizer opens registration and shares the code; players with an account ask to join (with a partner for team formats and an optional message), the organizer accepts or declines, then starts the tournament with the accepted players.

How do I join a tournament?

Tap Join Tournament and type the code the organizer shared (for example PD20-26A7-B3). If registration is open, sign in and tap Request to join; you'll see when you're accepted, also under My Tournaments.

What are co-admins?

Up to two helpers the organizer picks by username. They can accept requests, enter scores and advance stages — handy when the organizer is playing too.

How many teams or players can I add?

Groups tournaments go up to 64 teams in the knockout; Americano takes 4 to 40 players.

How are standings calculated?

Standings update automatically as you enter results — ranked by wins first or by total points, which you choose when creating the tournament.

Can I change my username, name or photo?

Yes: Profile → Edit profile for the name and username, and Add / Change / Remove photo under your picture.

Someone is offensive — how do I report or block them?

Tap ⋯ next to their name (on a tournament or a join request) and choose Report or Block. Blocked players can't ask to join your tournaments or add you as a co-admin. We review reports within 24 hours. Unblock under Profile → Blocked users.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open the app and tap your picture (top corner of Home).
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

This deletes your profile, username, photo, email, birth year, your tournament list, your join requests and your sign-in. Tournaments you organized stay for the players who took part, without your username, and can't be edited any more. Can't open the app? Email mostafa-m-sayed@hotmail.com from your account's email address and we'll delete it for you. See the Privacy Policy.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Email mostafa-m-sayed@hotmail.com with a description of what happened and, if possible, steps to reproduce it.
